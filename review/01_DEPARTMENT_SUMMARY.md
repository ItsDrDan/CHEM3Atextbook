# Revised CHEM 3A open textbook: summary for department review

**Request:** approve *Introductory Chemistry, Atoms First for FCC* (revised edition) as the CHEM 3A text under the COR's substitution clause ("other suitable OER texts selected by the teaching faculty and approved by the Chemistry Department may be substituted"), and move it into the live FCC book during a term gap.

**Read it:** the draft is private until release, so a signed-out link shows "You do not have permission to view this page." Either add each reviewer to the Conductor project (https://commons.libretexts.org/projects/DTvJm7TcCY; they sign in with a free LibreOne account) and send the Workbench link, https://chem.libretexts.org/Workbench/Introductory_Chemistry_Atoms_First_for_FCC_-_Revision_Draft_DTvJm7TcCY, or send them the compiled PDF. Start with the Instructor Guide in the Front Matter.

**Lead author:** Daniel Kwasniewski, adapted from *Introductory Chemistry, Atoms First for FCC* (compiled by Seth Yates, LibreTexts, 2025), the OER the COR lists now.

## What it is

- **Scope:** the CHEM 3A COR effective Spring 2027 (4 units, C-ID CHEM 101), nothing added to the main path and nothing left out. A few topics beyond the COR sit on pages labeled *Going Further* (6.11 resonance and octet exceptions; 7.12 redox in acidic/basic solution and batteries; 13.8 Ka, Kb, Ksp), so a section can assign them without changing what the course tests.
- **Order:** COR order, with one deliberate exception: measurement (COR unit 3) opens the book in Chapter 1, because the course opens with it.
- **Audience:** about 75% of CHEM 3A students are headed for allied health. Examples, Connection boxes, and a running patient case (Mateo) that returns in several chapters are chosen for them, with biology, engineering, and Central Valley contexts too.
- **Reference sheets:** every value matches the yellow and green sheets used on exams. Calculator steps are written for the TI-36X Pro.

## What changed from the current OER

- Every page is new or rewritten. The last 12 inherited pages (Chapter 1 measurement, from CK-12 and Ball, under NC and NC-SA licenses) were rewritten on 2026-11-17, so **the whole book is now CC BY 4.0**.
- Chapters were reordered to follow the new COR; superseded pages were moved out of the book (kept privately, not deleted).
- Every exercise gained a Hint; worked examples use one consistent format; the practice tools above are new.
- Science checks: every chapter was reviewed with numbers recomputed (all molar masses against the yellow sheet; 560 reaction equations balanced by script; all nuclear equations checked). Findings and fixes are logged in `tools/ISSUES_LOG.md`.

## Cost, access, and licensing

- **Free** online and as a downloadable PDF; it qualifies for the schedule's zero-textbook-cost (ZTC) marking if the ADAPT homework is also free to students (ADAPT is free).
- **License:** text CC BY 4.0. OpenStax figures and PhET simulations are CC BY 4.0 with credits. The ItsDrDan videos, games, and interactive labs are linked, not part of the licensed text (all rights reserved, free to use).
- **Remixing:** any instructor can copy chapters into their own LibreTexts book, or use the ADAPT questions in their own assignments.

## Accessibility

Built to WCAG 2.1 AA. The Accessibility Conformance Report (Front Matter) rates **41 of 50 criteria Supports, 4 Partially supports, 5 Not applicable**. The partial ratings come from the lack of separate audio-description tracks for the optional videos and from LibreTexts' own glossary pop-ups, which the book cannot change. Automated tests covered every page at phone width (320 px). A screen-reader listening test is still to be done.
