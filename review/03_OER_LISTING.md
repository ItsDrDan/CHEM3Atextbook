A free, phone-ready introductory chemistry textbook for allied health, science, and engineering students, written for the CHEM 3A course outline at Fresno City College (C-ID CHEM 101). Every exercise has a hint and a worked answer; Try It boxes, interactive labs, captioned video lessons, and ADAPT homework are built in.

## Long description (about 150 words)

*Introductory Chemistry, Atoms First for FCC* covers a one-semester introductory general chemistry course (C-ID CHEM 101) in 14 chapters, from measurement and atomic structure through bonding, reactions, stoichiometry, gases, solutions, acids and bases, equilibrium, and nuclear chemistry. It follows the Fresno City College CHEM 3A course outline and is written for students headed into nursing, dental hygiene, radiologic technology, respiratory therapy, biology, and engineering, with clinical and Central Valley examples and a running patient case.

Each section teaches through worked examples in a Problem / Solution / Check format, Professor Tips, and Common Mistakes. All 1,398 exercises and practice problems have a hint and a worked answer. Auto-graded Try It boxes explain every answer choice, Explore It boxes embed interactive labs and PhET simulations, and 107 short video lessons are captioned. Each chapter has a Chapter Guide with key equations and terms, and an ADAPT homework assignment. An Instructor Guide and an Accessibility Conformance Report are included.
