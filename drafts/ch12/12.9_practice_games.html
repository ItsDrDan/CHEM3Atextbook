How to use this page

These games are free, work on your phone, and keep score in your browser. Play a few rounds after each class; a short session every day beats one long one before the exam. Each game also opens in a new tab if you prefer a bigger screen.

(classify, strong vs. weak, a particle beaker with a light bulb), pH Lab (indicators including red cabbage), pH Calculator (typed pH, pOH, [H3O+], [OH−]), Proton Pairs (Arrhenius and Brønsted–Lowry, conjugate pairs with proton arrows), Titration Lab, Buffer Lab (with patient cases), and a 10-question Skill Check.
