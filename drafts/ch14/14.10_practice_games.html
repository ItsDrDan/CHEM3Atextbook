How to use this page

These games and practice sets are free, work on your phone, and keep score in your browser. Play a few rounds after each class; a short session every day beats one long one before the exam. Each one also opens in a new tab if you prefer a bigger screen. There is no separate nuclear chemistry game yet, so this chapter's practice is in Final Boss and in the Try It sets below.
