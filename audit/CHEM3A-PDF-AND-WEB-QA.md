## Visual sweep coverage

- **Contact sheets viewed (30 pages each):** pp. 1–150, 181–210, 241–270, 331–360, 511–540, 571–600, 691–720, 781–810. That is about **390 of 850 active pages**, and every chapter has at least its opener and Chapter Guide, a calculation page and a Practice Problems page in a viewed sheet or in a targeted render.
- **Targeted full-size renders:** pp. 17, 23, 26, 37, 81, 132, plus about 22 Chapter 1 pages rendered by the Chapter 1 reviewer.
- **Results:** consistent callout styles, readable tables, and no clipped figures, overlapping text or lost reaction arrows in the viewed pages. YouTube embeds print as "This interactive element is not available in this format" + link + QR code. Try It boxes print their title, description and "Open this practice in a new tab" link, so each one is usable in print.
- **Not viewed:** contact sheets for pp. 151–180, 211–240, 271–330, 361–510, 541–570, 601–690, 721–780 and 811–850. Their text was checked by the reviewers and the automated scans (raw TeX, "Math Processing Error", "curated by .", placeholders): **0 hits** in the active pages.
