# Chapter 14 (Radioactivity and Nuclear Chemistry) — science and answer-key audit

Coverage: Read every page in full from the drafts HTML (14.0 intro and 14.1–14.10). That covers 23 worked examples, 24 section exercises and all 55 practice problems (14.1–14.55), plus all 75 Try It items (ch14.json) and all 42 ADAPT items (ch14_adapt.json). I checked the A and Z balance of every nuclear equation in the text, the problems and both banks, including every distractor nuclide against the error its feedback names: alpha, beta, positron, gamma/metastable, electron capture, bombardment (Al-27/α, N-14/α, N-14/n, Be-9/α, Te-124/p,2n, O-18/p,n, B-10/n, Mo-98/n,γ), fission with neutron coefficients, fusion, and the U-238 → Ra-226 series (3 α, 2 β). I recomputed every half-life item: n = t/t½ is a whole number in all of them, and I checked the amount, activity, time, half-life and back-calculation, plus the distractor values (÷2n, n+1, ×n). Every radiocarbon age uses 5730 y and is reported to 3 sf (11,460 → 1.15 × 10⁴; 17,190 → 1.72 × 10⁴; 22,920 → 2.29 × 10⁴; 34,380 → 3.44 × 10⁴; 40,110 → 4.01 × 10⁴; 45,840 → 4.58 × 10⁴), and none uses 5 half-lives. I also recomputed the Ci/Bq/MBq/μCi/pCi conversions, rem/Sv/mSv, inverse-square dose rates, radon decays per liter, and the Diablo Canyon total. Half-lives in the tables and banks (F-18, Tc-99m, I-123, I-131, Rn-222, P-32, Co-60, Na-24, Bi-210, Ra-223, Sm-153, Ga-67, In-111, Xe-133, Cr-51, Lu-177, Ir-192, H-3, K-40, U-238) match reference values. Note on the banks: the correct choice is always index 0, and `k` is the calculator flag. Every keyed answer, explanation and per-choice feedback agrees, and I found no numeric or balancing errors.

Health and imaging claims checked: units (activity vs dose); the 10 CFR 20.1201 limit of 50 mSv (5 rem) per year; background of about 3 mSv and about 6.2 mSv total; dental, panoramic and chest X-ray doses; the 2024 JADA thyroid-collar statement; EPA radon figures (21,000 deaths, 4.0 pCi/L); radiosensitivity of dividing cells; acute radiation sickness at about 1 Sv or more; FDG trapping and fasting; I-131 beta range and ATA precautions; Gamma Knife; sterilization and food irradiation not making products radioactive; X-ray vs gamma sources. All are accurate and cautious, with "context only" disclaimers. I found no dosing or treatment advice.
