**Overall.** Chapter 11 is already dense and close to his deck. Every COR 9 item has a section, there are worked examples for each calculation type, and the IV, saline and serum-osmolality ties are in. The real gaps are:
- **His problem-solving technique,** missing from the teaching text:
  - "start with the term by itself";
  - "start on the side where you know both V and M";
  - the "which strategy?" decision chart;
  - writing givens under the equation.
- **Three worked-example types the teaching text never shows:**
  - ion concentration from a dissociation equation;
  - limiting reactant with two solutions;
  - percent used "backward" as a conversion factor.
- **His analogies:** Goldilocks for saturation, Kool-Aid for dilution, the kid at the sleepover for boiling-point elevation, and the hyper five-year-old for gas solubility.
- **One dilution decision problem:** which stock can you dilute?

No whole new page is needed. I propose two short H2 subsections.
