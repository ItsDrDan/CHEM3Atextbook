# Remix comparison: CHEM 3A draft vs. other open intro/GOB chemistry books

Written 2026-11-17. Research only; no draft pages were changed. All licenses below were checked on the live pages on 2026-11-17.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **OpenStax changed its license.** On openstax.org, *Chemistry 2e* and *Chemistry: Atoms First 2e* now carry **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. Their prefaces also say the books may not be used to train large language models without OpenStax's permission. The **LibreTexts copies, imported from the earlier CC BY releases, are still tagged CC BY 4.0**, page by page (checked on 9.1 Gas Pressure, 11.4 Colligative Properties, and 14.6 Buffers). A CC license cannot be withdrawn from a version already released under it. So: **remix only from the LibreTexts copies**, attribute "OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, CC BY 4.0", and do not copy from the current openstax.org pages.
   - [Chemistry 2e (OpenStax), LibreTexts](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/General_Chemistry/Chemistry_2e_(OpenStax))
   - [Chemistry: Atoms First 2e (OpenStax), LibreTexts](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/General_Chemistry/Chemistry_-_Atoms_First_2e_(OpenStax))
2. **Most "intro chem" books on LibreTexts are not CC BY.**
   - *Introductory Chemistry (LibreTexts)* (Alviar-Agnew and Agnew; the old Tro map) has a "CC BY" tag on its front page. Its own Detailed Licensing page tells a different story: 79.1% of its pages are **CK-12 License**, 12.6% are CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, and only 4 of 191 pages are CC BY 4.0.
   - The Ball books are NC-SA. *Chemistry for Allied Health (Soult)* is CK-12.
   - **The only large, adoption-heavy CC BY source is OpenStax (LibreTexts copy).** *Introduction to General Chemistry (Malik)* is **Public Domain** and is the best health-leaning source with no restrictions.
3. **Our book is already ahead on pedagogy.** We have more of almost every feature: worked examples with a Check step, Common Mistakes, Professor Tips, about 250 auto-graded Try Its, every practice answer with reasoning (OpenStax gives odd-numbered answers only), timestamped videos, ADAPT, a patient case, and Valley and allied-health boxes.
   - **Our clear gap is figures.** The drafts hold only **3 to 10 images per chapter**, 92 in all. An OpenStax chapter has 30 or more. OpenStax's CC BY art, especially its health and everyday photos and its labeled diagrams, is the highest-value thing to remix.

## 5. Where ours is already clearly stronger

- **Feedback.** Every practice answer comes with reasoning, and Try It widgets give per-choice right/wrong feedback. OpenStax gives odd answers only; Ball gives selected answers.
- **Worked examples.** Problem / Solution / **Check**, plus Common Mistakes and Professor Tips. OpenStax has Check Your Learning, but no error analysis.
- **Health depth.** Four allied-health box types (Nursing, Dental Hygiene, Rad Tech, Respiratory Therapy), a running patient case (Mateo), Biology Connections, and Valley boxes with sources. Ball's "To Your Health" is the closest rival, and it is NC-SA and dated 2012.
- **Video spine.** Timestamped ItsDrDan video tables in every guide. No competitor has this.
- **Homework.** About 50 ADAPT questions per chapter with rotating sets, and 40–60 practice problems per chapter.
- **Scope fit.** COR-aligned, with reference-sheet conventions applied everywhere (yellow/green sheets, the sig-fig rule, TI-36X Pro tips). OpenStax overshoots CHEM 3A; the CK-12-based books undershoot.
- **Phone accessibility.** `data-th` tables, `<details>` answers, scroll-safe equations.
