This report states how the book meets the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at levels A and AA, criterion by criterion, in the style of a Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT). It is meant for disability services offices, adoption committees, and instructors who need the details behind the shorter Accessibility Statement.

Scope

  • Covered: every page of this book (__PAGES__ pages: front matter, chapter introductions, sections, Chapter Guides, Practice Problems, Practice Games pages), the Try It practice boxes inside the pages, and the PDF version.
  • Platform: the book is hosted on LibreTexts. LibreTexts' own header, menus, search, glossary pop-ups, and Previous/Next controls are outside the book's control; where they affect a criterion, the remarks say so.
  • Embedded, tested separately: the ItsDrDan study games and interactive labs appear in Explore It boxes and on the Practice Games pages. They are optional practice, and each section teaches the same skills in accessible text. The conformance table below rates the book's own pages and Try It boxes, not the games. On November 20, 2026, the opening screen of all 83 embedded game and lab frames passed an automated WCAG 2.1 A/AA scan (axe-core) at phone width, and keyboard focus moved into and out of each frame. Later game steps were not scanned, the games have not had a screen-reader listening test, and some games use animation or single-key shortcuts.
  • Linked, not covered: the PhET simulations, YouTube's player, and the ADAPT homework platform are separate products with their own accessibility; they are optional or have equivalents in the text, as noted below.

How the Book Was Tested

  • An automated WCAG 2.1 A/AA scan (axe-core) of every live page, at a screen width of 320 pixels (the width WCAG uses for 400% zoom), with every hidden hint and answer opened and the math rendered.
  • A check on every page for content wider than the screen at 320 pixels.
  • A source check of every page: alternative text on every image (__IMGS__), header cells on every data table (__TABLES__), a title on every embedded frame (__IFRAMES__), headings that never skip a level, and no vague link text.
  • A check of the caption tracks on all __VIDEOS__ embedded videos.
  • A check of what a screen reader announces: the spoken text MathJax generates for equations, and the names, roles, selected state, and live feedback of the Try It boxes.
  • Keyboard-only and touch testing of the Try It boxes (including on an iPhone simulator), and contrast measurement of their colors and focus ring.
  • A check of the PDF's tags (headings, figures with alternative text, table headers, document language).
