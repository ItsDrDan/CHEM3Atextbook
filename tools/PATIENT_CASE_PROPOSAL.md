## A (recommended): Mateo, an adult with asthma in the San Joaquin Valley

**Who:** Mateo Herrera, 34, an irrigation technician on a Fresno County almond orchard, with moderate persistent asthma. Over the semester he has a bad week: wildfire smoke and a high-ozone day trigger an attack, he is seen in the emergency department, admitted overnight, and later has a lung scan.
**Why it fits:** respiratory therapy is the natural lead (gases, partial pressure, Henry's law, blood pH, Le Chatelier with CO₂), with strong roles for nursing (dosing math, IV saline, electrolytes), radiologic technology (chest X-ray, V/Q scan with Tc-99m) and dental hygiene (inhaler use and dry mouth, caries risk, fluoride). The Valley tie is real and well documented: the San Joaquin Valley has some of the worst ozone and fine-particle air pollution in the U.S. (American Lung Association, *State of the Air*), and asthma rates in Valley counties are high (California Department of Public Health).
